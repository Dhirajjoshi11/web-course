About Our Bakery-Café

Welcome to Leipomo-kahvila Voisilmä, a friendly bakery-café in the centre of Seinäjoki, Finland. We believe that good food, fresh baking and friendly service make every visit special.

Freshly Baked Every Morning

We prepare our baked goods every morning using carefully selected ingredients. Our selection includes sweet pastries, favourites and delicious treats.

Allergen Information

We understand that food allergies and dietary requirements are important.

Our menu provides information about these common allergens:

  • Gluten
  • Lactose
  • Nuts
  • Eggs

A Place to Enjoy

Take a moment to relax, enjoy freshly baked treats and share good moments with friends and family. We look forward to making every visit special.
