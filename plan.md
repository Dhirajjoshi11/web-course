
#  Plan

## 1. Goal

The goal of this website is to create a simple and welcoming online presence for Leipomo-kahvila Voisilmä, a bakery-café in the centre of Seinäjoki.
The website will help customers find information about the café, explore the menu, learn about allergens and check opening hours. It will also provide a contact page where customers can send a cake order request.
I want the website to be easy to use, clear to read and suitable for different visitors.

## 2. Target Visitors

The website is mainly for people who live in or visit Seinäjoki.

The main visitors include:

- People who want to find a local bakery-café.
- Customers who want to see the menu and prices.
- People who need information about food allergens.
- Customers who want to check opening hours.
- People who are interested in ordering a cake for a special occasion.

The information should be easy to find so visitors do not have to spend too much time searching.

## 4. Website Structure

The website will have four HTML pages.

### Home – index.html
- Introduce Leipomo-kahvila Voisilmä.
- Give visitors a short overview of the bakery-café.
- Show the opening hours.

### About Us – about.html
- Introduce the bakery-café.
- Explain its focus on fresh baking.
- Provide information about allergens.

### Our Menu – listing.html
- Display sweet pastries and savoury products.
- Show product names and prices.
- Include allergen checkboxes for visitors.

### Contact – contact.html
- Show the café's location and opening hours.
- Include a cake order request form.
- Allow customers to provide their contact details and cake preferences.

## 5. Design

The website will have a simple and consistent structure across all four pages.
The planned colours are:
- Dark green:#1f3d2b
- Cream:#f7f3e8
- Mustard yellow:#c8902e

I also want to make the website easy to read and navigate. I will use clear headings, descriptive text, semantic HTML elements and properly labelled form fields.
